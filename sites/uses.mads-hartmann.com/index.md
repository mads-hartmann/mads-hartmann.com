## Photography

I shoot with a **Fuji x100v**. I use the [Lens hood LH-X100](https://fujifilm-x.com/global/products/accessories/lh-x100/) which also includes the Adapter ring AR-X100. I have mounted a **Fujifilm x100v Folding Thumbrest by Lensmate** in the hot shoe; I found this to provide additional grip without making the camera too bulky. For filters I use **Urth Magnetic Essentials Filter Kit Plus+ (49mm)**

For storage I use a **Lexar Professional 1066x SDHC/SDXC kort - R160/W70 - 256 GB**.

I use **Larmor (GGS) Fujifilm x100v LCD protector ultra thin optical glass** to protect the LCD display against scratches.

I print my photos using a **Canon Selphy CP1300** printer.
